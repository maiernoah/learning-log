About this log

This is where I keep what I'm learning. Most entries link to an exhibit: a small interactive page I built to work through one idea. The note under each entry says what the page is and what it taught me.

The newest entries are at the top of the front page. Topics and Archives in the sidebar lead to older ones, and Find at the top searches titles, notes, and topics.

How it's made

The exhibits were built with Claude. The log itself is plain HTML, one stylesheet, and a little JavaScript. It sets no cookies and runs no trackers.
