📜 How the scores work

Each gift gets a score out of 100. About two-thirds of it is how much Noah would probably love it. The rest is how gift-friendly the price is: $15 to $100 is the sweet spot, and the score drops for very cheap things and for big-ticket items.

90+ Santa's pick 75–89 Very nice 60–74 Nice Under 60 Stocking stuffer, maybe

Prices were checked around November 10, 2026 and will change. Amazon prices come from Noah's saved Amazon lists; blank prices mean we couldn't check. Used and limited-edition copies can sell out.

Before you buy, it's worth checking with someone in the know so Noah doesn't get two of the same book. 🦌
