Three things: where to send the dossier, which organization, and anything the organization has given you that the public record would not show. $25 for Standard or $50 for Deep. 1. Your email

The finished dossier is sent here. Nothing else is.

2. The organization's EIN

Nine digits. Every U.S. nonprofit has one; it is on the organization's donate page, its Form 990, or ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer. The EIN, not the name, is what keeps two organizations with similar names apart.

3. Internal documents

Optional. Audits, annual reports, strategic plans, board decks, grant proposals or reports the organization has shared with you. PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text, up to 10 MB each. Documents are read only for your dossier and are not published.

    Organizations and readers may request a correction to any factual claim. Corrections that check out are made and noted on the page.

    Request received. Noah will confirm the organization and the price by email before any work starts. Keep your documents; if the request needs anything more, the email will say so.

    What happens next

    1. Noah confirms the organization behind the EIN and the price with you by email.
    2. The dossier is compiled: public filings, the public record, what the organization has actually changed, its field, its leadership, four futures and a verdict. Your documents are read alongside the public record and cited as supplied.
    3. You receive one self-contained web page you can open anywhere, print, or save as a PDF, with every claim tagged and sourced.

    The fine print

    Dossiers are compiled from public records by research agents and arithmetic on filed numbers. Every claim is tagged evidence, inference or speculation and carries its sources. Nothing in a dossier is legal, financial, tax or investment advice, and the organization assessed has not reviewed it. Verify anything you intend to act on.
